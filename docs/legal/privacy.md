# Privacy Policy

Version {{POLICY_VERSION}} · effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

Lattice makes weekly class timetables for colleges. It is run by {{OPERATOR_NAME}} ("we"). This page explains what
personal data Lattice handles, why, where it goes and what you can do about it. Questions or complaints go to
{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

## If you don't sign in

You can use Lattice without an account.

- The data you enter (week setup, departments, teachers, rooms, sections, courses, timetables) is saved **only in
  your browser**. We never receive it.
- When you click *Generate timetable*, your browser sends that data to our solver server to build the timetable.
  Before sending, it replaces every teacher's name with a code, so the solver receives no names. The solver sends
  the timetable back and keeps nothing: it doesn't store or log what you send.

## If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It lets you create or join an institute and work on it with others.

**What we store**

| Data | Where it comes from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your email address | You, or your Google account | To sign you in and match you to invites |
| Your name and profile picture link | Your Google account, if you sign in with Google | To show who you are to other members of your institutes |
| The institutes you create or join, and your role in each (admin, editor, viewer) | You and your institute's admin | To decide what you can see and change |
| Invites: an email address and a role | Typed in by an institute's admin | So that person joins the institute when they sign in |
| When you accepted these terms, and which version | You | To show you agreed, and to ask again when they change |
| Sign-in records (time, IP address, browser) | Kept by our sign-in provider | Security, and to stop abuse |

We use this data **only to run Lattice**: signing you in, managing institutes and members, and making timetables.
We don't sell it, show ads, or use it for anything else.

**Who can see it**

- The admin of an institute sees the name and email of each of its members, and the institute's pending invites.
- Editors and viewers see the admin's name and email, so they know whom to ask.
- We can see everything stored, to run and fix the service.

**Invites.** If an admin invites you, we store your email address before you have signed up. We don't email you;
the admin tells you to sign in. An unused invite is deleted after 30 days, or earlier if the admin cancels it.

## Where your data is kept

- **Supabase** stores accounts and institutes in its Mumbai region (Amazon Web Services, India). It works for us
  under its [data processing terms](https://supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/data-processing-addendum) and uses
  these [subprocessors](https://supabase.com/legal/subprocessor-list/June-1-2026.pdf) [check: link the current
  list from Supabase's Legal Hub rather than a dated PDF].
- **Google** handles the sign-in if you choose *Sign in with Google*. We receive only your name, email address
  and profile picture link. We use them only as described above, never for ads, and we don't share them.
- Our **solver server** receives timetable data without teacher names and keeps nothing.

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules let each person reach only their own institutes' data.

## Browser storage

Lattice saves your timetable data and your sign-in session in your browser's storage. It uses no advertising or
tracking cookies and no analytics.

## How long we keep it

- Your account and memberships: until you delete your account.
- Invites: until they're used or cancelled, and never more than 30 days.

## Your rights

You can:

- **Delete your account** from the account menu (*Delete account…*). An institute's admin must first make
  someone else admin or delete the institute. Deleting your account also withdraws your consent.
- **Ask for a summary** of the personal data we hold about you, and who we've shared it with, by writing to
  {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
- **Correct your data.** Your name comes from your Google account; change it there and it updates the next time
  you sign in. For anything else, write to us.
- **Name someone** who may use these rights for you if you die or can't act yourself, by writing to us.
- **Complain** to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data
  Protection Board of India.

## Age

Lattice is for college staff. You must be 18 or older to sign in.

## Changes to this policy

When we change what we collect or why, we publish a new version here and ask you to accept it the next time you
use Lattice signed in. Small fixes, such as typos, don't change the version.
